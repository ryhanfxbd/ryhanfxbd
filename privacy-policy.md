# BD LIVE TV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

BD LIVE TV ("the app") is developed by RYHAN HOSSEN. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## Information we collect

- **Account information.** If you create an account or sign in, we store your email address and a user ID through Firebase Authentication, together with your subscription plan (Free, VIP or Premium) so the app can unlock the right channels.
- **Anonymous usage statistics.** The app sends an anonymous heartbeat (a random install ID, first/last use dates, open count and app version) so we can count how many people use the app. This contains no name, email or device identifier.
- **Preferences stored on your device.** Settings such as your last watched channel and player options stay on your device.

## What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, messages, precise location, or payment card details. The app contains no advertising SDKs and we never sell personal information.

## How information is used

Account and plan data are used only to sign you in and apply your plan. Anonymous statistics are used only to understand overall usage. Channel lists and stream addresses are loaded from our public configuration files; the streams themselves are provided by third-party broadcasters and CDNs, which may have their own policies.

## Data sharing

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) to store account, plan and configuration data. Data is not shared with any other third party except as required by law.

## Your choices

You can use the app as a guest without an account. To delete your account and associated data, email us and we will remove it.
